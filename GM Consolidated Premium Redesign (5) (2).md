## ABOUT GM CONSOLIDATED

Established in 1983, G.M. Consolidated is an elite appliance design,

development, and engineering manufacturer. Over four decades of

operational expertise has made us a premier partner for small domestic

appliances and personal care electronics across the nation.

We pride ourselves on manufacturing high-performance, export-quality

products engineered to exceed industry baselines. Our corporate philosophy

centers around design precision, clean modern aesthetics, and failure-proof

product durability metrics.

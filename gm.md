**Heading:** Our Journey

**Intro paragraph:**

**Second paragraph:**
Since 1983, GM Group has been a pioneer in aluminum pressure die casting, transforming into a leading OEM provider of innovative dry irons, steam irons, and accessories for renowned global brands in India. Our unwavering commitment to excellence and technological advancement has propelled us to the forefront of the industry. By seamlessly integrating advanced tooling and plastic injection molding, we deliver superior products that consistently exceed customer expectations.

| Year | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| 1983 | Casting and coating for automobile & appliance sector. |
| 1994 | Development of dryers and thermostat. |
| 1996 | Started manufacturing small domestic appliances - Delhi. |
| 1998 | Set up a plant for Irons (Pioneered the use of conveyor belts). |
| 2000 | Diversification into kitchen appliances with sandwich toasters and hand toasters. |
| 2004 | Setting up plants in Kaaleamb, with complete backward integration for greater quality control. |
| 2007 | Expanded to Steam Iron Assembly. |
| 2012 | Establishment of R&D department for design and development as an ODM. |
| 2016 | Establishment of Rao Industries to meet the increasing demand. |
| 2019 | Added multiple assembly lines for new products like water heaters, room heaters and injection molding. |
| 2021 | Introducing a varied product range. |
| 2022 | India's first fully indigenous steam iron. |
| 2024 | Expanded in personal care appliances. |
